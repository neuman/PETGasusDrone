# Inspiration: two organic printed quad frames (photo from the author, 2026-10-17)

The author shared a photo of two white quadcopter frames on a wooden table as the
direction to aim for. The photo is not copied here (its owner is unknown); this is
what it shows.

- Matte white, slightly grainy surface: likely powder-bed nylon (SLS or MJF), not FDM.
- No flat plates. Every member is a rounded, tapered limb that blends into the
  next with large fillets, like bone.
- Each arm branches: a main limb runs from the body to a round motor pad with a
  4-hole pattern and a centre hole, and a second limb curves down and out into a
  partial prop guard: an open arc under and outside the prop, not a closed duct.
- The guard arcs and arms meet the body at several points, so the arms are
  triangulated in 3D rather than cantilevered from one joint.
- The body is an open cage with large oval windows, a raised loop (a strap or
  battery slot) across the top, and two small upright tabs at the front.
- Embossed text on the arm limbs. Sizes are not given; the larger frame looks
  like a 3-inch class and the smaller a 2.5-inch class.
