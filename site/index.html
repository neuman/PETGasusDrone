Why the frame looks like this

We asked a computer where the plastic should go

Printed drone frames have a reputation for shaking. A stiffer frame shakes less, but plastic costs weight, and weight costs flying time. So we gave a topology optimizer the problem: here are the motors, the ducts and the board. Put the plastic only where it does the most good.

plastic the design chose (flat, 2 mm thick) a raised rib on top (5 mm tall: most of the stiffness) the same in every version: ducts, motor seats, center plate the computer's answer: darker = more plastic
