## 2026-10-07 — Plate layout drawn from the topology optimiser

`plate-layout-drawn-from-the-topology-optimiser`

The base plate is the optimiser's load path made printable: no hub-to-motor arms; each motor seat hangs on 4 ribbed spokes (1.3 mm x 4.8 mm ribs) from its own duct ring, the hub reaches the four duct-to-duct webs on ribbed bridges, and the webs are 6 mm tall. Judged by the same 2.5D plate FE on the exact printed layout: first mode 286 Hz vs 187 Hz for the hand-drawn arms at the same webs, crash compliance 2.2x lower, for +0.8 g.

**Rejected**

- **hand-drawn arms + 3 flat spokes** — first mode 187 Hz, under C4's 200 Hz
- **optimiser density field printed directly** — its spokes were 0.75 mm blades; the 1.6 mm minimum-member clean-up deleted them and cut the motor seats loose (first mode 7 Hz)
- **5 spokes, as the optimiser drew** — thrust-to-weight 3.025: no margin on C1
- **3 spokes** — crash stiffness 0.85-1.18x the hand-drawn frame's
- **4 mm webs** — first mode 213 Hz; 6 mm gives 283 Hz for 0.12 g

**Params:** `plate_layout`, `spoke_count`, `spoke_phase_deg`, `web_h`  
**Claims:** `C4`, `C1`  
**Evidence:** `build/topopt/field.png`
